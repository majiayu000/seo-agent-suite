## Boundaries

State measured, inferred, unknown, or not in scope for indexing, rankings,
keyword volume, backlinks, and AI visibility. List manual/auth/data blockers
only for the conclusions they prevent. Crawl or content readiness alone does
not establish any of those outcomes.

## Next Actions
